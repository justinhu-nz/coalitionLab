Method. Among parties that clear the threshold (≥ threshold% of the party vote or ≥ 1 electorate seat when the coat-tail rule is on), the nominal seats are handed out one at a time to the highest quotient votes / (2s + 1) (Sainte-Laguë odd divisors 1, 3, 5, …). A party's list seats = entitlement − electorate seats. If a party wins more electorates than its entitlement, it keeps them all and the surplus is added on top as overhang, growing Parliament beyond the nominal total — as with Te Pāti Māori in 2023. Party lists are assumed long enough to fill every list seat (the rare list-exhaustion "underhang" case is not modelled). Not affiliated with the Electoral Commission — a projection tool, not official.
