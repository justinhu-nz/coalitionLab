electorateLab

2026 electorates, forecast from 2023 results at every voting place

Party vote (national)

Each seat's party vote moves by the national change. Electorate votes follow how each party's voters split in 2023, plus each seat's own candidate effect.

Assumptions

Swing: 1 = uniform (every seat moves the same number of points), 0 = proportional. Errors are one standard deviation, scaled by vote size. Redrawn and open seats get 25% more seat error.

How it works

1. 2023 on 2026 boundaries. Every 2023 voting place is placed inside its 2025 electorate. Votes cast elsewhere (special, overseas, out-of-area) are shared across each old seat's new pieces in proportion.
2. Party vote in each seat moves with the national poll.
3. Electorate vote = 2023 split-voting (e.g. 62% of ACT voters backed the National candidate) applied to the new party vote, plus that seat's 2023 candidate effect. Retiring MPs take part of their effect with them, and parties with no candidate lose theirs.
4. Probabilities come from 4,000 simulated elections, with a shared national poll error and local noise in each seat.
