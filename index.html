Special votes

How this works

A pattern is each party’s share of special votes divided by its election-night share, for each election, adjusted to 2026’s 26% overseas share (2011–23 average: National 0.84×, Labour 1.07×, Greens 1.54×, TOP 1.51×). The Electoral Commission estimated 399,365 specials; in years when enrolment also closed early, about 92% counted. Electorate leaders don’t move with this: flip them under Close seats.
