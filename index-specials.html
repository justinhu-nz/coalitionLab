Ratios

A ratio is a party's share among special votes divided by its election-night share: 1.5× means half as much again. Ticks mark each election from 2002 to 2023, with the NZ and overseas ratios reweighted to 2026's 26% overseas share; click a year to snap to it. Disallowed votes were 2.1–2.6% in 2020–23 and 6–9% before; election-day enrolment is gone in 2026, so expect something nearer the older figures. Applied to 2026's shares the ratios add to more than 100%, so renormalising (on by default) scales them back; turn it off to see the raw ratios.

Seats use the calculator's Sainte-Laguë engine: 5% threshold, one-electorate coat-tail, overhang kept. Electorate leaders come from the count and do not change with specials. TOP has no ratio before 2017 and Te Pāti Māori none for 2002; those years use the 2011–23 average for that party. Others have no history and stay at 1× unless you change them.
